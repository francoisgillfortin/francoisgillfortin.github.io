About the Artist

Biography

IMERSAO is a solo live music project, rooted in an organic trance style with influences from around the world. On stage, handpan, didgeridoo and percussion are played simultaneously, in a performance guided by rhythm and breath.


Between the jungle and the dancefloor, IMERSAO makes bodies move and minds travel, blending ancestral soundscapes with electronic pulses. A live experience to be felt and lived.
