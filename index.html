About the Artist

Biography

Photo: Danylo Bobyk

IMERSAO is a solo live music project by multi-instrumentalist François Gill-Fortin, rooted in Organic Trance and inspired by world music. Through handpan, didgeridoo and percussion, IMERSAO creates immersive journeys where rhythm, breath and movement come together in a fully live performance.


At the heart of IMERSAO is the belief that music is a tool for presence and connection. Improvisation plays an essential role in the creative process, allowing each performance to become a unique moment shared between the artist, the audience and the energy of the present.


Between the jungle and the dancefloor, IMERSAO blends ancestral sounds with modern electronic pulses, inviting the body to move and the mind to travel.
